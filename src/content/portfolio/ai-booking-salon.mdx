## The Challenge
Maison Éclat, a high-end salon on Linking Road in Bandra, was struggling to manage over 65 daily appointment requests. During styling rush hours, the front desk missed nearly 30% of calls. Furthermore, an 18% no-show rate caused severe revenue leakage for their senior stylists.

## The Architectural Solution
SHREEJAN AI SOLUTIONS designed and deployed an end-to-end conversational AI booking ecosystem:
- **WhatsApp Cloud API Integration:** Built a custom RAG-powered agent that handles customer inquiries in English and Hindi, explains complex hair treatments, and checks live stylist availability.
- **Dynamic Slot Locking:** Direct integration with Google Calendar and local POS systems with automatic 15-minute buffer intervals for chair sanitation.
- **Micro-Deposit Gateway:** Automated Razorpay UPI link generation to secure bookings with instant receipt generation.
- **Automated Reminder Webhooks:** 2-hour pre-appointment reminders containing navigation directions and parking assistance.

## Key Outcomes
Within 90 days of launch, client no-shows dropped from 18.4% to 2.1%, while after-hours bookings generated over ₹3,84,000 in incremental monthly gross billings.
