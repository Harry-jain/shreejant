## The Challenge
Kavya Couture, an ethnic luxury pret brand headquartered in Mumbai, was experiencing severe conversion drop-offs on their legacy WooCommerce store. Over 82% of their traffic arrived via mobile devices, where heavy product images and checkout friction caused a high 76% cart abandonment rate.

## The Architectural Solution
SHREEJAN AI SOLUTIONS completely rebuilt the digital infrastructure as a headless Next.js application:
- **Headless Next.js App Router Architecture:** Server-rendered product listings and edge-cached catalog data delivering instantaneous page loads across Mumbai, Delhi, and Bangalore.
- **1-Click UPI Checkout:** Embedded zero-friction checkout removing mandatory 5-step form fields, enabling customers to pay in under 20 seconds.
- **AI Size & Fit Engine:** Built an interactive micro-survey predicting exact saree blouse and lehenga sizing with 97% accuracy.
- **Automated WhatsApp Logistics:** Real-time dispatch alerts linked to Shiprocket with live tracking links sent automatically.

## Key Outcomes
Mobile conversion rate jumped by 42% in the first 60 days, while returns attributed to incorrect sizing declined by 31%.
