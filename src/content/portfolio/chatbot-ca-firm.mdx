## The Challenge
Mehta & Associates, an established CA firm in Fort, Mumbai, was overwhelmed during peak tax filing seasons by hundreds of repetitive client emails and WhatsApp messages asking for deadline updates, required checklist documents, and tax slab calculations.

## The Architectural Solution
SHREEJAN AI SOLUTIONS designed and deployed an institutional digital ecosystem:
- **Cinzel & Inter Corporate Web Portal:** A crisp, authoritative web presence reflecting four decades of financial precision and institutional trust.
- **RAG Tax Assistant:** Trained on the latest Indian Union Budget circulars, GST council notifications, and common compliance queries to provide instant, cited answers.
- **Automated Document Collection Bot:** Directs clients to upload Form 16, Bank Statements, and TDS certificates with automated verification of file format, image resolution, and PAN readability.

## Key Outcomes
The firm saved over 28 partner and associate hours every week during peak tax season, while client satisfaction scores reached a record 98%.
