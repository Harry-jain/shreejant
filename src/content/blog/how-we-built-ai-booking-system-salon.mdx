Luxury salons and wellness clinics in Mumbai face a unique operational paradox: high client demand during peak evening and weekend hours, coupled with front-desk staff overwhelmed by endless ringing phones and fragmented WhatsApp messages.

This case study examines how SHREEJAN AI SOLUTIONS transformed the appointment scheduling architecture for *Maison Éclat*, a premier hair and skin aesthetic salon situated on Linking Road, Bandra West.

### The Problem: Chaos at the Front Desk

Before partnering with SHREEJAN AI, the salon managed client appointments through a mix of physical registers, personal WhatsApp chats on multiple staff phones, and occasional phone calls.

The consequences were severe:
1. **Missed Inbound Bookings:** During peak salon styling hours (4 PM - 9 PM), the front desk frequently missed 15 to 20 client phone calls daily while billing in-salon clients.
2. **Double Bookings & Scheduling Conflicts:** Stylists were frequently double-booked for lengthy treatments (e.g., Balayage and Keratin therapies), leading to customer wait times and friction.
3. **High No-Show Rate:** Without automated calendar confirmations, approximately 18% of booked clients simply failed to appear, leaving high-earning stylists idle.
4. **Late Night Lost Revenue:** Over 40% of potential clients attempted to book appointments between 10:00 PM and 1:00 AM—hours when salon phones were completely unattended.

### The SHREEJAN Solution: A 24/7 Autonomous WhatsApp & Web Engine

Instead of forcing clients to download yet another cumbersome mobile app, we met them where they already spend their time: **WhatsApp**.

We engineered an integrated solution with three core components:

#### 1. Real-Time Calendar Synchronization Layer
We connected each stylist's individual chair availability, buffer times for sterilization, and service duration rules into a unified headless calendar engine.

#### 2. Multilingual Conversational AI Agent
We developed a specialized AI agent capable of understanding contextual salon inquiries:
- *"Can I book a senior stylist haircut and organic facial this Saturday around 5 PM?"*
- *"What is the difference between your Brazilian blowout and Kerafusion therapy?"*
- *"Can I reschedule my appointment from tomorrow 3 PM to Thursday 6 PM?"*

The agent retrieves real-time pricing, calculates open chair slots, and completes bookings in under 45 seconds without any human intervention.

#### 3. Automated Reminder & Deposit Collection Gateway
To eliminate no-shows, the system sends an automated WhatsApp confirmation with a 1-click Google Calendar add-link, followed by a polite interactive reminder 3 hours prior to the appointment. For high-ticket services, a ₹500 refundable deposit link is processed via UPI.
